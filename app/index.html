      How this actually works

      There are two different questions this tool can answer, and they're not the same question:

      • Safe Bet (Tier 1): "What wager can't hurt me if I'm right?" This assumes your opponents play the worst possible way against you — betting everything and getting it right — and finds the wager that beats that no matter what. It needs no guess about your confidence at all.
      • Smart Bet (Tier 2 / Daily Double engine): "Given how sure I actually am, and a guess at how opponents tend to bet, what wager gives me the best real odds of winning?" This one uses your confidence slider and can recommend a smaller, less paranoid wager than the Safe Bet when it's justified.

      The Smart Bet's opponent-behavior assumptions are honest placeholders, not numbers pulled from thousands of real games — see docs/adr/0002-opponent-model-assumptions.md in the project for the details. Treat its exact win-probability numbers as directionally useful, not gospel.

      The underlying math lives in Python at engine/fj_wager.py, engine/fj_bestresponse.py, engine/simulate.py, and engine/dd_wager.py, with tests in tests/. This page is a hand-written JavaScript port of that same logic so it can run instantly in a browser — it mirrors the Python engine but is not itself covered by the automated test suite yet.
